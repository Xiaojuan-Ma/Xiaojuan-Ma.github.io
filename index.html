About Me

Hi, this is Xiaojuan, a Ph.D. student in the Distributed Protocols Research Group (DPRG) at UIUC, working with Prof. Indranil Gupta . I am also working with Prof. Fernando Granha Jeronimo on quantum error correction and coding theory.

Before joining UIUC, I earned a Ph.D. in Physics from Beijing Normal University and a Master's degree in Computer Science from Iowa State University.

Research Interests

Quantum Networking Protocol design across quantum network layers
Quantum Computing Quantum error correction and coding theory
Broader interests: Distributed systems and networks · Systems for machine learning

Much of my research asks how local structure and constraints give rise to global behavior and information-processing capabilities. This perspective originated in my earlier Ph.D. work on complex networks, carried into my research on distributed systems and machine-learning systems, and now informs my work in quantum networking and quantum coding.

In quantum networking, I study how underlying network topology and hardware constraints interact with higher-layer routing, protocols, and applications. In quantum coding, I investigate how local algebraic and combinatorial structure shapes global code properties and error-correction guarantees.

Research

Quantum Networking

I study quantum networks across multiple layers, from physical entanglement connectivity to network protocols, routing, and distributed applications.

Quantum network topology · Entanglement distribution · Routing and scheduling · Distributed quantum protocols

Quantum Error Correction and Coding Theory

I study coding-theoretic questions motivated by fault-tolerant quantum computing, with a focus on structural properties that govern decoding and list-recovery guarantees.

Quantum error correction · CSS codes · Quantum list decoding · Quantum list recovery

Distributed Systems and Networks

My systems research studies how network structure and resource constraints affect distributed computation, communication, routing, and scheduling.

Distributed systems · Networked systems · Satellite networks · Edge systems

Systems for Machine Learning

I am also interested in systems techniques that exploit structure in distributed machine-learning workloads to improve efficiency and resource utilization.
