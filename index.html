About Me

Hi, this is Xiaojuan, a Ph.D. student in the Distributed Protocols Research Group (DPRG) at UIUC, working with Prof. Indranil Gupta . I also work with Prof. Fernando Granha Jeronimo on quantum error correction and coding theory.

Before joining UIUC, I earned a Ph.D. in Physics from Beijing Normal University and a Master's degree in Computer Science from Iowa State University.

Research Interests

Quantum Networking Protocol design across quantum network layers
Quantum Computing Quantum error correction and coding theory
Broader interests: Distributed systems and networks · Systems for machine learning

Much of my research asks how local structure and constraints give rise to global behavior and information-processing capabilities. This perspective originated in my earlier Ph.D. work on complex networks and has appeared throughout my subsequent research in distributed systems, machine-learning systems, quantum networking, and quantum coding.

In quantum networking, I study how underlying network topology and hardware constraints interact with higher-layer routing, protocols, and applications. In quantum coding, I investigate how local algebraic and combinatorial structure shapes global code properties and error-correction guarantees.

Research Experience

Quantum Networking

2021 – Present

I study topology-aware quantum networking, including network topology design, link-layer protocols, routing, and resource management across quantum network layers.

Network topology · Entanglement distribution · Link-layer protocols · Routing · Resource management

Quantum Error Correction and Coding Theory

Current

I study how local properties and constraints of quantum codes determine their global error-correction capabilities, with applications to coding-theoretic questions for fault-tolerant quantum computing.

CSS codes · Quantum list decoding · Quantum list recovery · qLDPC codes · Fault-tolerant quantum computing

Distributed Systems and Networks

2021 – Present

My systems research includes distributed protocols, congestion control and routing, satellite networking, and decentralized management of edge and IoT systems.

Distributed protocols · Satellite networks · Congestion control · Routing · Edge and IoT systems

Systems for Machine Learning

I study systems techniques for distributed machine-learning training, including parallelism and placement decisions constrained by computation graphs and device memory.

Distributed ML · Model parallelism · Memory-aware placement · Resource efficiency

Complex Networks

Earlier work

During my earlier Ph.D. work in physics, I studied how network topology and local interactions shape synchronization, information propagation, and collective dynamics in complex networks.

Complex networks · Network topology · Synchronization · Information propagation
