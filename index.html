About Me

Hi, this is Xiaojuan, a Ph.D. student in the Distributed Protocols Research Group (DPRG) at UIUC, advised by Prof. Indranil Gupta . I also work with Prof. Fernando Granha Jeronimo on quantum error correction and coding theory.

My current research interests focus on quantum networking and quantum error correction and coding theory for fault-tolerant quantum computing. I also have broad interests in classical networking, distributed systems, and systems for machine learning.

Before joining UIUC, I earned a Ph.D. in Physics from Beijing Normal University and a Master's degree in Computer Science from Iowa State University.

Research Interests

Current focus
Quantum networking: Protocol design across quantum network layers
Quantum computing: Quantum error correction and coding theory
Broader interests
Distributed systems and networks · Systems for machine learning

Much of my research asks how local structure and constraints give rise to global behavior and information-processing capabilities. This perspective originated in my earlier Ph.D. work on complex networks and has appeared throughout my subsequent research in distributed systems, machine-learning systems, quantum networking, and quantum coding.

In quantum networking, I study how underlying network topology and hardware constraints interact with higher-layer routing, protocols, and applications. In quantum coding, I investigate how local algebraic and combinatorial structure shapes global code properties and error-correction guarantees.
